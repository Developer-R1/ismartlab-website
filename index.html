Inauguration Highlights

Research Areas

Artificial Intelligence

AI-driven algorithms for dynamic traffic monitoring, automated signal control, and real-time transit optimization.

Data Driven

Leveraging spatial telemetry and large-scale origin-destination data to model complex urban transportation systems.

Data Science & Machine Learning

Predictive analytics for pavement performance, flow forecasting, and automated civil infrastructure health monitoring.

Electric Vehicles

EV integration studies, fleet charging demand analysis, and smart grid coordination for sustainable transit.

Innovative Research

Hardware-in-the-loop testing and experimental prototyping applied to modern transportation infrastructure.

Intellectual Collaboration

Fostering interdisciplinary partnerships between civil academia, industry leaders, and national research bodies.

Renewable Energy

Integrating solar power into highway microgrids and evaluating sustainable energy adoption in public transport.

Smart Solutions

Deploying intelligent transportation systems (ITS) to minimize congestion and lower urban mobility footprint.

Lab Equipments

01

Traffic Safety Cone

Heavy-duty reflective channelizing cones for highway safety field trials and lane-closure simulations.

02

Safety Gumboots

Used to protect the feet from water, mud, chemicals, and other workplace hazards.

03

Remidio InstaRef R20 Portable Autofractometer

Used to measure refractive error of the eye, including spherical and cylinderical errors, for vision assessment and screening.

04

Alcohol Detector/Breath Alcohol Analyzer

Used to detect and measure the presence of alcohol in a person's breath, particularly for checking alcohol consumption before driving or operating vehicles.

05

Safety Helmet / Hard Hat

Used to protect the head from impact and falling-object hazards at construction and field sites.

06

VBOX Racelogic GNSS Data Logger

Used for accurate measurement of vehicle speed, distance, position, acceleration, and vehicle-performance parameters during road and transportation testing.

07

High-Visibility Reflective Safety Vest

Used to increase the visibility and safety of workers, particularly during road, traffic, and field operations.

08

Industrial Safety Shoes

Used to protect the feet from impact, compression, puncture, and other workplace hazards.

09

Plastic Safety Chain

Used for barricading and demarcating restricted areas, work zones, and hazardous locations.

10

LED Traffic Control Baton

Used for traffic management, vehicle guidance, and signalling, particularly during night-time and low-visibility conditions.

11

Project Model

Perform micro-simulation testing, calibrate traffic volume counts, and compare queue waiting times against baseline static configurations.

12

Radar Gun

A radar gun measures the speed of moving objects by bouncing radio waves off them and using the doppler effect to calculate velocity.
